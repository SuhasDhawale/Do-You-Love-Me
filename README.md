# 💘 Do You Love Me?

A cute and playful animated webpage built with pure **HTML** and **CSS**.  
It asks the classic question: **"Do you love me?"** — and no matter what you choose (Love or No),  
you’ll always be met with a warm message:  
**“I love love love love so so much You!”** 💕

Made just for fun — to make someone smile. 🌸
